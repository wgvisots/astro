# Accuracy

Two kinds of evidence: the unit tests compare each component against an independent reference and
fail if the difference exceeds the stated tolerance; the studies in [`studies/`](https://github.com/wgvisots/astro/blob/main/studies)
measure the propagators and consistency checks end to end. Everything here can be reproduced (see
the end of the page).

## Validated against independent references (unit tests)

| Component | Reference | Test tolerance |
|---|---|---|
| Time scales UTC, TAI, TT, TDB, TCG, TCB, UT1, 1960-2035 incl. leap-second days | ERFA [ERFA](https://github.com/liberfa/erfa) | 1 ns |
| CIP X, Y (IAU 2006/2000A), 1900-2100 | ERFA `xy06` | 0.5 uas |
| CIO locator s | ERFA `s06` | 0.05 uas |
| Nutation dpsi / deps | ERFA `nut06a` | 5 / 1 uas (IERS tables and ERFA differ by a few uas at +-100 yr) |
| GAST | ERFA `gst06a` | 5 uas |
| GCRS -> ITRS rotation | ERFA `c2tcio` | 1 uas |
| IAU 2000B vs 2000A | the full model | 5 mas |
| ITRF -> GCRF, TEME, PEF | Vallado, Example 3-15 [Vallado (2013)](https://celestrak.org/software/vallado-sw.php) | 5 cm, 0.05 mm/s (the example uses IAU 2000) |
| Geodetic <-> ECEF | ERFA `gc2gd` near the surface; round trips everywhere | 2e-12 rad and 10 um; round trip 1e-8 m |
| ICRS <-> galactic | ERFA | 1e-14 rad |
| Refraction constants A, B | ERFA `refco` | 1e-15 |
| Observed place of a star (ICRS -> az/el) | ERFA `atco13` | 1 mas (1" below 10 deg elevation with refraction) |
| JPL SPK reader, both byte orders | `jplephem` on DE440s | 1e-12 relative + 1 mm |
| Low-precision Sun / Moon | DE440 | tested 0.01 deg / 0.1 deg; measured 1950-2050: Sun < 30", 6e-5 in distance; Moon < 6', 0.14% |
| SGP4 / SDP4 | Vallado's verification set, all cases [Vallado et al. (2006)](https://celestrak.org/publications/AIAA/2006-6753/) | 1 mm, 1 um/s (largest difference measured: 0.12 mm) |
| Kepler, elements, Lambert | Vallado Examples 2-4, 2-5, 7-5; Curtis Example 5.2 [Curtis](https://shop.elsevier.com/books/orbital-mechanics-for-engineering-students/curtis/978-0-12-824025-0) | about the printed precision (Kepler 1 m, 1 mm/s; Lambert 0.1-0.5 m/s) |
| Numerical J2/J4 | energy integral; Brouwer secular rates | energy 1e-10 over 10 days; node rate 0.3% |
| State transition matrix | finite differences of propagations; symplectic identity | 1e-6 relative; 1e-8 |
| Interpolated STM between steps | integration to the exact time | 2e-8 (default tolerance), 2e-6 (relTol 1e-9) |
| Covariance propagation | Monte Carlo: priors sampled from their covariance give chi-square distances | mean d^2 within 6 +- 0.6 (6-D, 400 samples), 6 +- 0.9 (elements, 4 h), 3 +- 0.45 (position, 4 h) |

The ERFA reference values are generated by [`tools/gen_reference.py`](https://github.com/wgvisots/astro/blob/main/tools/gen_reference.py)
and committed under `tests/data`.

## Numerical propagation

From `astro_study_propagation`. Worst position error over a LEO (400 km), a sun-synchronous orbit
(800 km, e = 0.01) and an eccentric orbit (1000 x 20000 km), against the same model integrated with
relTol 1e-14:

| relTol / absTol | Steps per 4 h (LEO) | Error after 4 h | Error after 1 day |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1e-12 / 1e-9 (default) | 158 | 0.23 mm | 2.9 mm |
| 1e-11 / 1e-8 | 119 | 2.2 mm | 3.6 cm |
| 1e-10 / 1e-7 | 90 | 2.0 cm | 0.39 m |
| 1e-9 / 1e-6 | 68 | 0.21 m | 4.9 m |
| 1e-8 / 1e-5 | 52 | 2.3 m | 60 m |
| 1e-7 / 1e-4 | 40 | 25 m | 710 m |

**Grid interpolation.** Over a day in LEO at the default tolerance, the grid output (natural ~96 s
steps, interpolated) is 2.46 mm from the reference, and the integrator alone at the end of the day
is also 2.46 mm off: the degree-7 Hermite interpolation adds nothing measurable. Stopping at every
60 s output instead forces smaller steps and gives 0.06 mm, at about 1.3x the cost.

**The J2 axis.** Applying J2/J4 about GCRS z instead of the true pole (0.150 deg apart on
2026-11-02) costs 56 m after 1 h and 273 m after 4 h in LEO. A fixed vector expressed in TOD of an
epoch and in TOD of date differs by 0.22 m after 1 h, 0.85 m after 4 h and 4.2 m after a day at LEO
radius (TEME: 0.19, 0.77, 4.5 m); `EstimateTracker` corrects for this to 1.4 mm.

## Analytic J2/J4 (secular)

Worst position error over 4 h against the numerical J2/J4 trajectory:

| Orbit | Elements taken from the state | Mean elements fitted to 2 orbits of states | Fit RMS |
|---|---|---|---|
| LEO 400 km | 117 km | 1.9 km | 1.1 km |
| SSO 800 km, e = 0.01 | 136 km | 5.9 km | 4.4 km |
| 1000 x 20000 km | 219 km | 3.8 km | 1.9 km |

A state's osculating elements are not mean elements, and the wrong mean motion makes the error grow
every orbit. Fitting removes that, but the short-period J2 terms a mean-element theory omits remain
at the kilometre level.

Velocity, `SecularPropagator` (the two-body velocity of the mean elements) against
`SecularPropagatorCorrected` (the time derivative of the same positions). Worst error over 4 h
against the numerical trajectory, and worst disagreement with dr/dt of the secular positions
themselves (central differences on a 1 s grid, which alone contribute ~0.002 m/s):

| Orbit | From the state: original / corrected | Fitted: original / corrected | \|v - dr/dt\|, fitted: original / corrected |
|---|---|---|---|
| LEO 400 km | 130.1 / 130.2 m/s | 6.25 / 3.59 m/s | 5.64 / 0.002 m/s |
| SSO 800 km, e = 0.01 | 136.6 / 136.6 m/s | 8.25 / 8.40 m/s | 9.03 / 0.001 m/s |
| 1000 x 20000 km | 39.5 / 39.3 m/s | 3.94 / 3.96 m/s | 1.15 / 0.001 m/s |

The correction makes velocity and position agree; it does not make the velocity much closer to the
truth, which the short-period terms dominate.

Starting `SecularPropagatorCorrected` from mean elements whose corrected velocity reproduces the
state's velocity at the epoch, instead of from the state's osculating elements (worst position
error over 4 h):

| Orbit | Osculating elements as mean | Velocity-matched mean elements |
|---|---|---|
| LEO 400 km | 117.0 km | 195.4 km |
| SSO 800 km, e = 0.01 | 136.1 km | 494.8 km |
| 1000 x 20000 km | 219.2 km | 153.8 km |
| near-equatorial, i = 0.5 deg | 21.9 km | 896.3 km |
| polar | 143.4 km | 570.9 km |
| retrograde, i = 140 deg | 87.4 km | 247.8 km |

The velocity-matched start is worse in five of six orbits, so the library does not offer it.

## Reproducing

```sh
cmake -B build -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Release -DASTRO_BUILD_STUDIES=ON
cmake --build build -j
ctest --test-dir build                       # the validation tests
build/studies/astro_study_propagation        # < 1 s
build/studies/astro_study_consistency        # ~1 min; optional argument: samples per row
build/studies/astro_study_impact             # ~2 s; optional argument: Monte Carlo samples
```

Regenerating the ERFA and JPL reference data needs Python with `numpy`, `pyerfa`, `sgp4` and
`jplephem` (`tools/gen_reference.py`, `tools/make_spk_fixture.py`).
